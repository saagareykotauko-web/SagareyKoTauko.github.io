Gaming Rulebook & Platform Guide

Official tournament standards and AI mechanics explained

Royal Ludo Engine Rules

• Rolling a 6 unlocks a token from yard or earns an extra roll.

• Landing on an opponent's token eliminates them back to their yard with victory taunts.

• Sockets marked with Stars (★) are safe zones where no captures can occur.

• Switch seamlessly between 1 Bot, 2 Bots, or 3 Bots mode.

Snakes & Ladders Rules

• Exact roll not mandatory until the finish zone at 100.

• Ladder bases immediately elevate tokens up to the apex.

• Snake heads swallow the token, sliding it down into the snake tail coordinate.

Tic Tac Toe Minimax Intelligence

• Bot mode calculates the recursive game tree (Minimax algorithm) guaranteeing it never loses a match.

• Perfect testing ground for human tactical defense.

Connect Four Block AI

• Drops tokens down lowest available slot in chosen column.

• AI scans 4-way vectors (horizontal, vertical, both diagonals) to instantly block 3-in-a-row threats.
